A SPRITE SHEET of ROOFTOP EQUIPMENT for the flat roofs of an isometric city map, drawn EXACTLY in the map's camera and style.
Camera: the same as ref1.png (a render of the map): orthographic, NO perspective, tilted 45° down, every object seen from the same angle as the buildings in ref1.png; the sun from the upper left as in ref1.png. Style: the clean illustrated look of ref1.png and ref2.png: flat-shaded surfaces, each one flat colour, a thin dark outline along every edge, soft even daylight. Mostly light greys, galvanised metal and white, as rooftop equipment is.
Scale: 16 pixels per metre (four times as large as in ref1.png), so small items keep their detail.

Draw these objects, each one separate with clear empty space (at least 40 px) around it, arranged in a loose grid, every object WHOLE (nothing cut off by the image edge):

5. a square duct running 4 m along the roof on low supports, with one bend
6. two mushroom ventilation cowls and one rotating turbine vent (each about 0.6 m)
7. a domed acrylic skylight (1.5 x 1.5 m), milky white-blue
8. a section of a long ridge skylight strip (6 m long, 1.6 m wide, gabled glass, metal frame)
9. a smoke-vent hatch with its flaps open (1.5 x 1.5 m)
10. a satellite dish on a small stand (1 m dish)
11. a TV antenna mast (3 m tall) with two antennas
12. a mobile phone mast: a 6 m lattice pole with three panel antennas and a small equipment cabinet at its foot
13. a small stair housing / roof access hut (3 x 2.5 m, 2.5 m tall) with a door, flat roof
14. a lift motor room (2.5 x 2.5 m, 2 m tall), plain plaster
15. a steel exhaust stack (0.5 m wide, 3 m tall) with guy wires
16. a roof hatch (1 x 1 m) and a short safety railing section (4 m)

Every object casts only a small soft contact shadow directly beneath itself, nothing long.
BACKGROUND: everything that is not an object is perfectly flat pure magenta #FF00FF: no shading, texture, shadows beyond the contact shadow, gradients, ground plane or outlines on it, and a sharp edge between each object and the magenta. No text, no labels, no numbers.
Output: one 2048x1536 image.
