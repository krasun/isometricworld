A SPRITE SHEET of construction-site objects for an isometric city map, drawn EXACTLY in the map's camera and style.
Camera: the same as ref1.png (a render of the map): orthographic, NO perspective, tilted 45° down, every object seen from the same angle as the buildings in ref1.png; the sun from the upper left as in ref1.png. Style: the clean illustrated look of ref1.png and ref2.png: flat-shaded surfaces, each one flat colour, a thin dark outline along every edge, soft even daylight.
Scale: 8 pixels per metre (twice as large as in ref1.png).

Draw these objects, each one separate with clear empty space (at least 40 px) around it, arranged in a loose grid, every object WHOLE (nothing cut off by the image edge):
1. a yellow tracked excavator (about 10 m long with its arm), arm stretched out to the right, bucket on the ground
2. the same excavator turned the other way (arm to the lower left)
3. a yellow wheel loader (about 7 m long)
4. a large orange-and-grey dump truck (about 9 m long), its body empty
5. a white concrete mixer truck (about 9 m long)
6. a stack of two white site-office containers (each 6 x 2.5 m, 2.6 m tall) with small windows and an outside stair
7. a single blue site container (6 x 2.5 m)
8. a red/rusty shipping container (12 x 2.5 m)
9. a row of three green portable toilets
10. a big heap of grey gravel (about 8 m across)
11. a heap of brown topsoil (about 8 m across)
12. a stack of large concrete pipes (about 6 m long)
13. a stack of steel beams / rebar bundles on timber blocks (about 8 m long)
14. a pallet stack of bricks / material under a tarp (about 3 m)

Every object casts only a small soft contact shadow directly beneath itself, nothing long.
BACKGROUND: everything that is not an object is perfectly flat pure magenta #FF00FF: no shading, texture, shadows beyond the contact shadow, gradients, ground plane or outlines on it, and a sharp edge between each object and the magenta. No text, no labels, no numbers.
Output: one 2048x1536 image.
